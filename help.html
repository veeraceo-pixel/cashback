Frequently Asked Questions

How does cashback work?▾
When you click through SmartCash to a partner store and make a purchase, we receive a commission from the retailer. We share the majority of that commission back with you as cashback. Your cashback is tracked automatically — no voucher codes needed.
How long does cashback take to appear?▾
Cashback typically appears as "Pending" within 24–72 hours. It becomes "Confirmed" once the retailer confirms the sale (usually 30–90 days, depending on their return policy). You can withdraw confirmed cashback within 24 hours of confirmation.
What is the minimum withdrawal?▾
Our minimum withdrawal is £10 — the lowest in the UK (most competitors require £25+). You can withdraw to your bank account, PayPal, or as an Amazon gift card. Payouts process within 24 hours.
My cashback isn't showing — what do I do?▾
First, check that you clicked through from SmartCash before purchasing. If you're sure you did, wait 72 hours as some retailers are slower to report. If it still hasn't appeared, contact us via the Help Centre with your order details and we'll investigate.
Is SmartCash free to use?▾
Completely free. We make money when retailers pay us commission — a portion of which we share with you. You never pay a fee. There's no subscription. No hidden charges.
How does Amazon order sync work?▾
When you connect your Amazon account via OAuth (read-only access), purchases you make through SmartCash links appear in both your Amazon order history and your SmartCash dashboard with live cashback status. We never have access to payment information.
What is the AI Shopping Advisor?▾
The AI Advisor is powered by Claude — one of the world's most capable AI models. You can ask it anything: "Best laptop under £800?", "Is now a good time to buy a PS5?", "Where gets me the best cashback for ASOS?" It gives real, intelligent answers — not keyword search results.
How do I earn XP and level up?▾
You earn XP every time you make a qualifying purchase through SmartCash. You start at Bronze (0 XP) and progress through Silver, Gold, Platinum and Diamond. Higher tiers earn bonus cashback of up to +2% on every purchase.
