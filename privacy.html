Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2026 · We are committed to protecting your privacy in line with UK GDPR.

1. Who We Are

SmartCash ("we", "us") operates the cashback platform at smartcash.co.uk. We are the data controller for the personal information you provide to us. Contact: privacy@smartcash.co.uk

2. What Data We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, password (hashed)
  • Transaction data: purchases, cashback amounts, withdrawal history
  • Click data: when you click through to partner stores
  • Device data: browser type, IP address, device identifiers (for fraud prevention)
  • Amazon data: order IDs and amounts (only if you connect your Amazon account via OAuth)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate your account and credit cashback
  • To process withdrawals
  • To detect and prevent fraud
  • To send transactional emails (cashback updates, withdrawal confirmations)
  • With your consent: marketing emails about new stores and offers

4. Legal Basis

We process your data under: contract performance (running your account), legitimate interests (fraud prevention, security), and consent (marketing). You may withdraw consent at any time.

5. Data Sharing

We share minimal data with: partner retailers (click IDs for tracking), payment processors (for withdrawals), and our infrastructure providers (Supabase). We never sell your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Your Rights

  • Access a copy of your data at any time
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Data portability
  • Lodge a complaint with the ICO at ico.org.uk

Exercise your rights by emailing privacy@smartcash.co.uk.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you close your account, we delete personal data within 90 days (except transaction records which we retain for 7 years for legal compliance).

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies for session management and security, and optional analytics cookies (with your consent) to improve the Platform. You can manage cookie preferences at any time via your browser settings.
