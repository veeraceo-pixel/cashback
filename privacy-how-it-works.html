Your Privacy & Data

Exactly what SmartCash does and doesn't know about you. We believe you deserve a plain-English answer — not a 40-page legal document.

  • Your Amazon, ASOS or any store password
  • Your payment card details
  • Your bank account information
  • What you browse on store websites
  • Your search history on any retailer
  • Your Amazon browsing behaviour

  • AI chat history (only within the session)
How cashback tracking actually works — step by step
1

You click "Shop Now" on SmartCash

We redirect you through our affiliate network (AWIN). This creates a small tracking cookie in your browser — a random reference code that says "this visit came from SmartCash". We never see what you do next.

Cookie: awinmid + clickref (anonymous random ID)
2

You shop normally on Amazon / ASOS / any store

You land directly on the retailer's website. You log in using the retailer's own login page — we are not involved in that login at all. Your username and password go directly to Amazon's servers, not through us. We literally cannot see them.

3

You complete a purchase

When your order is confirmed, the retailer's system detects the tracking cookie in your browser. The retailer (e.g. Amazon) sends a report to AWIN — our affiliate network. This report contains only: the order total, the commission amount, and our anonymous click reference. It does NOT include your name, address, items bought, or any personal data.

Amazon → AWIN: order_value=£79.99 | commission=£3.60 | clickref=SC_abc123
4

AWIN tells SmartCash about the sale

AWIN forwards to us: the order value, commission earned, transaction date, and your SmartCash click reference (which links back to your SmartCash account). That's it. We never receive the retailer's transaction ID, your order contents, or your delivery address.

5

We add cashback to your SmartCash balance

We calculate your cashback (e.g. 4.5% of £79.99 = £3.60) and add it to your SmartCash account as "Pending". After the retailer's return window closes (typically 30 days), it becomes "Confirmed" and you can withdraw it.

📦 The "Connect Amazon" feature — what it actually does

The "Connect Amazon" button uses Amazon's official OAuth system — the same technology used by Alexa, Kindle, and other Amazon-authorised apps. Here is exactly what happens:

1

You click "Connect Amazon". SmartCash sends you to Amazon's own login page (amazon.co.uk). You log in directly on Amazon's servers — we never see your credentials.

2

Amazon shows you a permissions screen: "SmartCash wants to access your order history". You choose whether to approve or deny. You can revoke this at any time in your Amazon account settings.

3

If you approve, Amazon gives SmartCash a read-only access token. This token lets us fetch your order list (items, dates, totals) so we can match orders against our cashback records. We cannot place orders, change your account, or access payment details with this token.

4

We display matching orders in your SmartCash dashboard alongside their cashback status. The raw order data is stored only as long as you keep your account connected.

To revoke access at any time: Go to Amazon → Account & Lists → Manage Your Content and Devices → Preferences → Manage Your Alexa Data / Apps & Services → SmartCash → Remove Access. Your order data is then deleted from our system within 24 hours.

Your rights under UK GDPR
📋

Right to access

Request a copy of all data we hold about you. We respond within 30 days, free of charge.

🗑️

Right to erasure

Ask us to delete your account and all associated data. We comply within 14 days (except transaction records required by HMRC for 7 years).

✏️

Right to rectification

Correct any inaccurate data we hold about you. Update most details directly in your dashboard.

📤

Right to portability

Request your data in a machine-readable format (JSON or CSV). Useful if you want to move to another cashback platform.

🚫

Right to object

Object to us processing your data for direct marketing at any time. Unsubscribe from any email with one click.

🔒

Right to restrict

Ask us to pause processing your data while a complaint is being resolved.
