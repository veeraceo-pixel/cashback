Our Story

We Built SmartCash
Because We Were Tired of
Leaving Money on the Table.

Every time we shopped online, we were paying full price when we didn't have to. Existing cashback sites were slow, complicated, and paid out only when they felt like it.

So we built SmartCash — combining automatic cashback tracking with Claude AI intelligence, real-time price history, and the UK's fastest 24-hour payout. We wanted something we'd actually use ourselves.
