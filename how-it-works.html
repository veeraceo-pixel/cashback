FAQ

Common Questions

Is SmartCash really free?+

Yes, 100% free forever. We earn a commission from retailers when you shop through us, and we share that with you as cashback. You never pay a penny.

How does Amazon order sync actually work?+

When you click to Amazon through SmartCash, our affiliate link tracks your purchase. If you've connected your Amazon account via OAuth, our system then matches your order IDs to your SmartCash clicks — so your orders appear in both places. Your Amazon account and shopping experience are unchanged.

Why didn't my cashback track?+

Common causes: you opened another tab after clicking our link; you had an ad blocker active; you were already logged into a retailer's app; or the item was excluded (e.g. gift cards). Always click directly from SmartCash with tracking enabled, and disable ad blockers for the retailer site.

When do I actually get paid?+

Cashback appears as "Pending" within 24–72 hours. It becomes "Confirmed" after the retailer's return window closes (usually 30 days). Once confirmed, you can withdraw any time — we process payments within 24 hours to your bank, PayPal, or Amazon gift card balance.

What is the AI Shopping Advisor?+

It's a conversational AI powered by Claude (Anthropic's AI). You can ask it anything — "find me a gaming chair under £200", "is this a good price for AirPods?", "when is the best time to buy a TV?". It knows about our store network, current cashback rates, and your price alert history.

How do I earn XP and level up?+

You earn 10 XP for every £1 of confirmed cashback. You also earn XP for signing up (100 XP), referring friends (50 XP each), setting price alerts (5 XP), and connecting your Amazon account (200 XP). Check your dashboard to track progress.
