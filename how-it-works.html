FAQ

Common Questions

Is SmartCash really free? +

Yes, 100% free forever. We earn a commission from retailers when you shop through us, and we pass that on to you as cashback. You never pay anything.

How does Amazon order sync work? +

When you click to Amazon through SmartCash, our affiliate link tracks your purchase. If you've connected your Amazon account, we match your order IDs to your SmartCash clicks — so orders appear in both places. Your Amazon experience is completely unchanged.

Why didn't my cashback track? +

Common causes: opening another tab after clicking our link; ad blocker active; already in the retailer's app; or excluded item (gift cards, etc.). Always click directly from SmartCash with ad blockers disabled on the retailer site.

When do I get paid? +

Cashback shows as "Pending" within 24–72 hours. It becomes "Confirmed" after the return window closes (usually 30 days). Then you can withdraw any time — we process within 24 hours to your bank, PayPal or Amazon gift card.

How do I earn XP and level up? +

You earn 10 XP per £1 of confirmed cashback. Also: 100 XP for signing up, 50 XP per referral, 200 XP for connecting Amazon, and 5 XP per price alert set. Track your progress in the dashboard.
